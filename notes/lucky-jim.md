## 2026-11-11 · pp. 0–20

The intro sets the scene that this book mainly came about from two young post WW2-era literary men feeling dissastified with the world and hating on it, which they turned into a lot of humor. That Jim is based on an amalgamation of these two authors in their youth. Apparently it was a huge hit upon publication.
